Community

The wall.

This is where the community lives: programs written in plain English, packages shared through jpm, and entries from the build challenges. Every entry links back to its author.

The wall is fresh paint. Be the first on it. Ship something in Jesun.Code, a package, a demo, a tutorial, and open an issue on GitHub with the link. The best entries get featured here with your name on them.

How to join

  • Star and fork the repo, then build.
  • Write your first program: show "hello, world" still counts. Write your tenth and it counts more.
  • Share a package with use "github.com/you/pkg" so others can bring in your work.
  • Prefer Bangla? use bangla switches all 67 keywords. The Bangladesh challenge entries land here.

House rules

  • Be kind. Review the code, never the person.
  • Plain English, everywhere: error messages, issues, and docs a newcomer can read.
  • No tracebacks, no leaked internals, no shortcuts. Ship it, verify it, log it.
