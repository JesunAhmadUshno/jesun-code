DEEPDELVE

Centuries ago the Deepdelve swallowed a kingdom and its gold. Delvers go down. Most do not come back.

You are the next delver. Fight goblins, rats and worse. Befriend the wanderers. Trade with the merchants. Slay the Wardens. Go deep.

Move: WASD / Arrows   Attack: bump into enemies or Space   Talk/Trade: E   Potion: Q

Death is permanent. Gold, depth and kills are your score.
