DEEPDELVE REALMS

Centuries ago the Deepdelve swallowed a kingdom and its gold. Above it, the village of Emberhold still keeps the lamps lit. Delvers go down. Most do not come back.

Every tile of this dungeon, every goblin, every gold coin is computed live in Jesun.Code, running in your browser. Press M underground for first-person sight.

Move: WASD / Arrows   Attack: bump or Space   Talk: E   Potion: Q   View: M   Pause: P
